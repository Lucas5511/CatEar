# Reconcile Brief — Perfect Ear

Purpose: check whether soft, non-structural ideas from `brief.md` (tone, differentiator narrative, the "perceived as hard like math" insight, the long-term audiação/capstone vision, unlockable surprises) survived translation into `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`. A rigid IA/component spine format tends to keep only what maps cleanly onto a surface, component, or state row — narrative/aspirational ideas that don't fit a table row can silently drop.

## What carried over well

- **"Perceived as hard like math" → anti-academic aesthetic.** This is the best-preserved thread. `DESIGN.md` directly operationalizes it: warm pastel palette, rounded shapes, "pet app not educational software" framing, explicit "Evitar... textura que remeta a partitura ou pauta musical," and a Do/Don't row banning corporate gray/blue. `EXPERIENCE.md`'s Inspiration & Anti-patterns section repeats the rejection of "apps tradicionais" aesthetics. Good fidelity.
- **Gentle, explanatory feedback / two-meter philosophy (effort never falls).** Brief's insight that skill oscillates naturally and shouldn't be punished is preserved almost verbatim in tone (Voice and Tone table, "Dia ruim" state row, banned "streak guilt" pattern).
- **"Genuine, measurable win" (baseline vs. day 1).** Preserved as its own IA surface (Progresso tab, Baseline comparison card) and dramatized in Key Flow 3 as a "climax" — good narrative fidelity to the brief's emphasis on this as a core hook.

## Gaps found

1. **Audiação as named capstone / top of skill tree — absent.** The brief is explicit that audiação is "a meta final nomeada do app, o topo do skill tree" (the long-term vision's headline structural detail). Neither spine's Skill Tree node component (`bloqueado/disponível/completo/em reforço`) nor the IA table leaves any placeholder for an aspirational/distant capstone node, nor any note that the tree's top is intentionally left open for it. Since this is explicitly "long-term vision, not v1 scope," it's reasonable it isn't built — but the spines don't even leave a structural or narrative hook (e.g., a note that the skill tree's top-end is reserved/open-ended) acknowledging where this eventually lands. Worth a one-line note so a future skill-tree redesign doesn't have to be retrofitted.

2. **"Resolução" (tension → release, cadência) concept — entirely missing.** Brief lists this as a V1 Should-Have with real narrative weight: it's explicitly framed as "ponte entre percepção intuitiva e a primeira composição própria do usuário" — i.e. it's not just a curriculum topic but the emotional/pedagogical bridge to the user's first act of composing. This doesn't appear anywhere in `DESIGN.md` or `EXPERIENCE.md` — no IA surface, no component, no mention in Key Flows or Voice and Tone. Given its Should-Have status and its narrative significance to the "feel like a real musician" job-to-be-done, this looks like a silent drop rather than a deliberate exclusion (unlike the explicitly-scoped-out "loot" surprises, which are named in the brief's out-of-scope list).

3. **Fading scaffolds (andaimes temporários — cor para consonância/dissonância) — entirely missing.** Also a V1 Should-Have, and one of the more distinctive pedagogical/UX devices in the brief (temporary associative aids like color-coding that deliberately fade as the "crutch" is no longer needed, always aiming at direct sound perception). Neither spine's Component Patterns, Colors, or State Patterns sections mention this mechanic or how fading would be represented visually/interactionally. This is a concrete, fairly unique product idea that has no home in either derived document.

4. **Long-term "surprise and discovery" vision (unlockable timbres, rare challenges) — not acknowledged as deferred.** Brief frames this as intentional out-of-v1-scope but part of the 2-3 year vision, explicitly contrasted with "loot"-style randomness that's rejected. `EXPERIENCE.md`'s Inspiration & Anti-patterns section rejects streak-punishment and traditional-app aesthetics but doesn't note this future direction at all — even a single line flagging "reserved for later, not designed here" would preserve the brief's intent that retention mechanics may evolve without compromising the pedagogy-first stance. Low priority since it's genuinely out of scope, but its total absence means a future reader of the UX spine alone wouldn't know this idea exists.

5. **Differentiator framing ("moat is execution + pedagogical rigor, not proprietary tech") — tone implication not explicit.** This is more a strategic/vision statement than a UX-actionable item, so its absence is defensible. But it does imply a specific tonal register: quiet confidence and craft rather than hype or gimmick marketing. Neither spine states this as a voice principle (the Voice and Tone table is all microcopy examples, no higher-level principle statement like "confident, unshowy, craft-forward"). Minor — flagging only because the brief calls this out as *the* differentiator, and voice/tone documents are exactly where such a principle would normally be codified.

## Assessment

No structural contradictions were found — where the brief's soft ideas map cleanly onto an IA surface or component (anti-academic tone, dual meters, baseline proof), the spines preserve them faithfully, sometimes with good narrative dramatization (Key Flows). The gaps are concentrated in ideas that don't map onto a single table row: two V1 Should-Have curriculum/pedagogy concepts ("resolução" and fading scaffolds) are missing outright rather than just under-specified, and the long-term audiação-as-capstone vision has no structural placeholder despite being named explicitly in the brief as the skill tree's eventual top node.
